Hierarchical Lead-Aware Multimodal ECG-RAG
Undergraduate Thesis · Q1 Target

Explainable Myocardial Infarction diagnosis on 12-lead Electrocardiograms, under Prof. Chen Xiaohe (School of Artificial Intelligence, CUPB).

  • Hierarchical Lead-Instance Pooling: Eliminates spatial lead-blindness in 12-lead ECGs by preserving anatomical territories (inferior, anterior, lateral).

Engineering Philosophy

I follow the Ponytail Principle: the cleanest code is often the code you didn't have to write. Prioritize standard libraries, native OS features, and minimal parameter additions over speculative bloat and fragile abstractions.
